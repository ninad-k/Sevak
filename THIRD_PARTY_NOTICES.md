## WordNet 3.0 (the bundled dictionary)

`crates/sevak-plugins/data/wordnet-en.z` is a trimmed copy of Princeton
University's WordNet 3.0 database: single words only, at most three meanings per
part of speech, definitions shortened and usage examples removed, plus WordNet's
lists of irregular word forms. It is generated by `scripts/build-dictionary.py`
and used offline by the `define` and `spell` keywords on systems without their
own dictionary.
