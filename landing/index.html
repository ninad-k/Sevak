How it works

Three keystrokes between you and anything

Sevak waits quietly in the background and appears where you are working the moment you call it.

1

Call it

Press Alt Space (or your own shortcut). The search bar opens on the screen you're using.

2

Type

A few letters of an app or file, a sum like 12*7, or a keyword such as g for Google.

3

Act

Enter launches, opens or copies. Ctrl K shows every action. Sevak hides and you keep working.

Features

Everything, one shortcut away

Built-in features cover the everyday jobs. Each one is a plugin you can switch off, and you can add your own.

Apps

Every installed app: Start Menu and Store apps, macOS apps, Linux desktop entries including Flatpak and Snap.

firecodecalc

Calculator & conversions

Answers as you type, and Enter copies them. Units of length, weight, temperature, data and more. Currency rates are optional.

12*75 km in mi72°F to C

Web search

Short keywords for Google, YouTube and GitHub. Add any site with a {query} URL.

g rust traitsytgh

Files & folders

Finds files in the folders you choose, and browses any path as you type, with Tab to complete.

f report~/Downloads/

Browser bookmarks

Chrome, Edge, Brave, Vivaldi, Opera, Firefox and more, merged across browsers and profiles.

b recipes

Clipboard history

Search and paste anything you copied earlier. Off until you turn it on, and stored only on your computer.

cb

Snippets

Paste saved text such as signatures and replies, with placeholders like {date} and {clipboard}.

s sig

System & shell

Lock, sleep, restart or empty the trash, with a confirmation for anything destructive. Run commands in your terminal.

locksleep> git status

Universal Actions

Select text in any app and press Ctrl Alt Space: search it, change its case, encode or decode it, or show it in Large Type.

Script plugins

Extend Sevak with Python, Node.js or PowerShell scripts. Compatible with Alfred's Script Filter format.

Make it yours

Light, dark or system theme, your accent colour, fonts, custom CSS, and global hotkeys for any search or action.

Private by design

Searching apps and files, calculations and ranking all run on your computer. Nothing is collected about you.

Why Sevak

Small, quick and respectful of your machine

The same launcher on every computer you use, and it stays out of your way.

Instant

Built in Rust with Tauri. Results update with every keystroke, and Sevak waits in the background so it appears right away.

Learns your habits

What you open often and recently rises to the top, and Sevak remembers which result you picked for each search.

Truly cross-platform

Windows 10 and 11, macOS 11 and later, and Linux on both X11 and Wayland, including GNOME's Wayland session.

Your data stays yours

No account and no telemetry. Sevak only goes online when you run a web search, check for updates (optional), or turn on currency rates.

A closer look

Designed to disappear

A single search bar that grows only as much as it needs to, with a Settings window for everything else.

सेवक
The name
Sevak (सेवक) means one who serves.

That's the whole design brief. It appears when you call it, does what you ask, and gets out of the way. The flame-and-lamp logo carries the same idea: readiness to help, and a light to work by.
