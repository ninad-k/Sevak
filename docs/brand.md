# The name and identity of Sevak

![Sevak logo: an amber S-shaped flame and dark wordmark](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ninad-k/Sevak/main/assets/brand/sevak-logo-concept.png)

## Meaning

**Sevak** (सेवक) means **one who serves**. It is a Sanskrit-derived word used
in Hindi for a person who serves or assists.
[Source: Hindwi dictionary](https://www.hindwi.org/hindi-dictionary/meaning-of-sevak).

For this app, the name expresses a design intention: bring useful actions
within reach, respond to a few keystrokes, and let the user return to work.

## The logo

The mark combines an **S-shaped flame** with a shallow lamp. The flame suggests
clarity and direction; the lamp suggests readiness to help. These are intended
design associations, not claims about the dictionary meaning.

Amber gives the mark warmth and contrast against indigo. The compact symbol
appears in the app icon, tray and search bar; the wide wordmark suits
documentation and larger placements.

## Product descriptions

**Tagline**

> Your desktop. At your service.

**Short repository description**

> A keyboard-first desktop launcher for Windows, macOS and Linux. Find apps and files, calculate, and search the web from one bar.

**Long description**

> Sevak brings everyday desktop actions into one keyboard-driven search bar.
> Open it with a configurable shortcut, type an app name, filename,
> calculation or web keyword, and press Enter to act. Local search and usage
> ranking keep common tasks close at hand, while settings and built-in
> plugins let you choose the folders, engines and features you need.
